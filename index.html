About

Bennett Teitle

I am a mechanical engineering student at Duke University's Pratt School of Engineering pursuing a certificate in aerospace engineering. My interests center on aerospace systems, hypersonic propulsion, computational analysis, and AI in engineering design.

My technical work includes using various CFD codes including OPENFOAM, VULCAN and SU2 on Linux-based systems and HPC clusters, developing multiprocessing Python workflows for CFD post-processing, performing 3D flow-field analysis in ParaView/Tecplot and applying tools including ANSYS, Dyrobes, SolidWorks, MATLAB, Python, and Java.
