About

About me.

I am a mechanical engineering student at Duke University's Pratt School of Engineering pursuing a certificate in aerospace engineering. My interests center on aerospace systems, hypersonics, propulsion, computational analysis, and hands-on engineering design.

I have research experience at the Air Force Research Laboratory across the High Speed Systems Division and Turbine Engine Division, where I have worked on computational hypersonics, CFD workflows, high-performance computing, LES visualization, and structural analysis for aerospace propulsion systems.

My technical work includes using NASA's VULCAN CFD code on Linux-based HPC clusters, developing multiprocessing Python workflows for CFD post-processing, performing 3D flow-field analysis in ParaView, and applying tools including ANSYS, Dyrobes, SolidWorks, MATLAB, Python, Java, and embedded C++.

Outside of research, I build across engineering, aviation, and media. I hold a Private Pilot License, have hands-on aircraft construction experience through PNB Aviation and EAA Chapter 75, and have led technical and creative projects spanning embedded systems, video production, web design, and student organization leadership.
